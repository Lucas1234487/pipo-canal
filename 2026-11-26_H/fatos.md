## Regra (ficção declarada)
"Regra nova: todo bicho ganhou olho na frente, igual o seu." (linha1). Ficção, não fato. Só muda bicho de olho do lado (pombo, vaca, galinha, cavalo, coelho); gato, cachorro e coruja ficam iguais (linha2), porque já têm olho voltado pra frente (fato c).

## a) Bicho de olho do lado tem campo de visão bem largo, quase tudo em volta (linha7: "O pombo de verdade tem olho do lado: vê quase tudo em volta.")
1. https://www.birdnote.org/listen/shows/pigeon-eyed-view-world
   - "eyeballs located laterally, on the sides of the head"
   - "a remarkable, 340-degree view without moving its head, including a wide area behind its head."
2. https://theconversation.com/curious-kids-how-do-birds-see-where-theyre-going-101932
   - pombos: "field of vision, of about 300 degrees"
   - "Having a wider field of vision with only a small blind spot behind them"
3. Apoio (cavalo): https://www.popsci.com/animals/why-do-horses-have-eyes-on-the-side-of-their-head/
   - "Horses' side-set eyes give them a wide field of vision—an advantage when scanning the environment for predators."
   - "Monocular and binocular vision combined gives them close to 350-degree vision around their body."
4. Apoio (cavalo): https://www.dvm360.com/view/vision-in-dogs-and-cats - "Horses are estimated to have a visual field of about 355°"
5. Apoio (pombo/coelho): https://www.10000birds.com/ask-a-birder-why-do-some-birds-have-forward-facing-eyes-while-others-have-sideways-ones.htm - "a pigeon can watch you approach from behind without moving its head." / https://www.sciencefocus.com/nature/why-dont-prey-animals-have-eyes-in-the-backs-of-their-heads/ - "Rabbits, for instance, have eyes high up on the side of the head."
NÚMERO: as fontes do pombo NÃO batem (BirdNote 340; The Conversation cerca de 300; 10000 Birds "almost 340" mas para aves de olho do lado em geral). Por isso a linha7 fica SEM número. "Quase tudo em volta" não exagera nenhuma delas.

## b) Olho na frente (humano, gato): menos dos lados, ponto cego atrás, ganha profundidade (linha8: "Olho na frente deixa um ponto cego enorme nas costas.")
1. https://www.dvm360.com/view/vision-in-dogs-and-cats
   - "Species with front-facing eyes have a large binocular visual field."
   - "Consequently, they have 2 small lateral monocular visual fields and a large posterior blind spot."
   - "Frontal eyes are found in predator species that require superior depth perception"
   - gato: "The total visual field of cats is estimated at 200°, including a 140° frontal binocular field"
2. https://www.vin.com/apputil/content/defaultadv1.aspx?pId=11132&id=3844144 (Paul E. Miller, oftalmologista veterinário)
   - "Most dogs probably have 30-60° of binocular overlap (versus approximately 140° cats and humans)."
   - campo total: cão 240°, gato 200°, humano 180° (ou seja, sobra o resto, atrás, sem ver)
3. Apoio: https://theconversation.com/curious-kids-how-do-birds-see-where-theyre-going-101932 - "Because owls have eyes at the front of their heads, they have a smaller field of vision"
4. Apoio: 10000 Birds (acima) - "binocular vision greatly supports judging distances, something that is important when catching prey."
"Ponto cego enorme nas costas" = "large posterior blind spot" (dvm360). Não exagera.

## c) Gato, cachorro e coruja já têm olho voltado pra frente; cachorro é menos frontal que o gato (linha2: "Gato, cachorro e coruja nem notaram: já eram assim.")

2. https://www.10000birds.com/ask-a-birder-why-do-some-birds-have-forward-facing-eyes-while-others-have-sideways-ones.htm - "Owls are actually the family that takes the forward-oriented eyes to extremes"
3. Cachorro menos frontal que gato: VIN (acima) - "Most dogs probably have 30-60° of binocular overlap (versus approximately 140° cats and humans)." e https://www.vetstreet.com/our-pet-experts/pet-vision-vs-people-vision-who-sees-more - "cats are similar with an estimated range of 200 degrees" / "the average dog, which can see an incredible 240 degrees around himself."
A fala não diz que o cachorro é tão frontal quanto o gato; só que ele não muda com a regra (já tem olho voltado pra frente). Nenhum número na fala.
